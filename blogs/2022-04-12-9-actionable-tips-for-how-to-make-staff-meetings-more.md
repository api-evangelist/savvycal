If it feels like the number of team meeting invites in your inbox has skyrocketed since the entire world collectively went remote in March 2020, that’s because it has. With so much time spent on a webcam and in Zoom , it’s time for managers and team leads to learn how to plan team meetings that both capture employee attention and encourage participation. In this post, we’re sharing 9 actionable tips to help founders and people managers run more engaging staff meetings.
