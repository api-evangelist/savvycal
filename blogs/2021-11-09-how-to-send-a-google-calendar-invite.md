If you are like most of us, you rely on your calendar for everything from keeping track of work meetings and calls to personal appointments, parties, and important errands. We’re guessing if you are reading this that your primary calendar app is Google Calendar. So, scheduling and keeping track of all of your emails and calendar events (and sending meeting invitations) is an important and largely unavoidable productivity task.
