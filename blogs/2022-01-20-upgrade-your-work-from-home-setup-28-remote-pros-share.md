A healthy and habitable office environment is a must-have when you work from home. Creating a dedicated space (with the freedom to roam) and designing a setup conducive to your best work can boost your performance and encourage behaviors that lead to more focus and follow-through. It can also help you set better boundaries between your world of work and your world of play, relaxation, and personal responsibilities.
