Whether you’re new to working remotely or you’ve been working remotely for years, it’s time to revisit our Zoom etiquette now that the rest of the world is following suit. If you’re new to remote work, it’s important to adopt the right video call rules and best practices so you can productively collaborate with others. And especially for the remote work veterans out there, you may have picked up some bad habits that will cause Zoom fatigue and hurt yours and others’ experiences on calls.
