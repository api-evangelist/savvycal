What if there was one activity you could do each week that is proven to increase company and employee performance and have a positive impact on company culture? There is—it is having regular 1 on 1 meetings with your team. However, with the average busy manager spending 32.9 hours each week in meetings , according to a Reclaim.ai 2021 study, and Zoom fatigue being a real phenomenon, too often the manager’s direct reports pay the price.
