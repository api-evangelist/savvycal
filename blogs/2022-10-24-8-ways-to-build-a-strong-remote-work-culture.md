Remote work has certainly seen an uptick in recent years with 20% of the workforce working fully remote and, unsurprisingly, 40% of job seekers stating that they would prefer to work remotely. With this significant part of the workforce working fully remote, challenges sometimes arise when trying to keep a strong remote work culture. Employees seek companies where they feel happy to work.
