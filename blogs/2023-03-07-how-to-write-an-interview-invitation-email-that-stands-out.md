When it comes to the hiring process, your interview invitation email might seem like a small detail in the overall candidate experience, but it can make a huge impact on a candidate’s perception of their new potential employer and their excitement throughout the job interview process. After all, this is often one of the first things a candidate will receive from your company. It sets the tone for the rest of the interview process.
