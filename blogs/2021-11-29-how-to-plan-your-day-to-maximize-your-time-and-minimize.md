Starting your day without a clear purpose is a catalyst for procrastination. Because rather than simply having to execute an existing plan, you waste time making decisions about what you feel like doing rather than focusing on planned activities. Without some intentionality behind your daily actions — your priorities, purpose, and any hope of making progress plunge into a pit of distractions that can derail even the best of us.
