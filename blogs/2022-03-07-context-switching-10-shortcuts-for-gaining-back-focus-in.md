Being able to effectively context switch is an underrated skill. Mainly because it’s almost impossible to avoid rapid context switching when you work with a team, work from home, or simply have a mind that tends to muddle with many problems at once. And while you’ll find most articles tell you how it’s killing your productivity and multitasking is the devil (both true!) — I’d argue it’s more helpful to see how some context switching can be a good thing.
